# AI Sales Analyst V4 — Product North Star

## Ultimate goal
Turn the existing AI Sales Analyst into a polished, robust, dynamic, end-user-friendly SaaS product that businesses will pay for repeatedly.

## Product promise
Upload sales/revenue data -> understand the business -> explain what changed -> explain why -> identify risks/opportunities -> recommend actions -> support grounded Q&A and drill-down -> produce executive reports.

## Long-term monetization path
1. Trial/free analysis
2. Pro analyst workspace
3. Team/workspace plans
4. Recurring intelligence, alerts, scheduled reports
5. Connectors and data integrations
6. Usage limits, authentication, billing and subscriptions

## Do not regress
The project must not return to Streamlit-centric page/widget state management. Streamlit V3 remains a frozen analytical reference, not the V4 SaaS frontend.

## Analytical trust
- Deterministic calculations own all numbers.
- Metric definitions are canonical and reusable.
- Every important insight has evidence.
- The AI explains validated results; it does not manufacture results.
- Unsupported business metrics are explicitly unavailable.

## Core workspaces
Overview | Explore | Insights | Ask | Actions | Reports

## Reference patterns
Salesforce: decision-oriented revenue intelligence
HubSpot: guided sales analysis and action
Tableau Pulse: metrics, proactive insights and explanation
ThoughtSpot: search-driven exploration
Metabase: governed AI analytics and reuse of existing answers
Lightdash/WrenAI: semantic context and governed analytical meaning
