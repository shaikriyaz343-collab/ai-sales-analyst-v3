# V4 UAE Launch Pack — 2026-09-22

This is a launch-operations pack derived from the current V4 UAE/Dubai go-to-market strategy. It is preparation material only and does not change the frozen production release candidate.

## 1. Beachhead

Primary market: United Arab Emirates, beginning with Dubai.

Initial target: B2B companies with 10–100 employees, roughly 5–30 salespeople, approximately $1M–$20M annual revenue as a qualification heuristic, an existing sales dataset in CSV/XLSX, CRM export, or a simple operational system, a founder, Head of Sales, or COO involved in recurring revenue review, limited analyst/RevOps capacity, and a clear need to understand pipeline and revenue without a long implementation project.

Priority verticals:
1. B2B professional services
2. Technology / managed services
3. Recruitment and staffing
4. Commercial real estate / property services
5. B2B distribution / wholesale

## 2. Positioning

Primary: Your AI Revenue Analyst, without replacing your CRM.

Supporting: Upload your sales data. Get the revenue story, evidence-backed risks, forecast, and next actions.

Core decision loop: What changed → Why → Forecast → Attention → Action → Evidence

Avoid presenting the product as generic BI or as a replacement CRM.

## 3. Offer

Starter: $49/month.

Growth: $149/month.

Trial: 14 days.

First commercial conversation: Give us your latest sales export. We’ll show you what changed, what deserves attention, and why.

## 4. First-customer motion

Qualified account → data upload → first useful decision → repeat analysis → paid conversion

For the first 5 customer candidates, record ICP fit, data uploaded, time-to-first-decision, whether the decision was accepted or acted on, second analysis / repeat visit, monitoring or saved-intelligence use, trial-to-paid outcome, and conversion or non-conversion reason.

Do not optimize for traffic volume before the activation path is observable.

## 5. 15-minute walkthrough

Minute 0–3 — Context: ask how the prospect currently reviews sales, where the data lives, and how much preparation is required.

Minute 3–6 — Upload: use samples/uae_b2b_demo_pipeline.csv or a sanitized prospect export.

Minute 6–10 — Decision loop: show what changed, why it matters, what the data supports for the forecast, what deserves attention, and what evidence supports the conclusion.

Minute 10–13 — Follow-up: ask one natural question in the analyst and save one useful finding.

Minute 13–15 — Trial: invite the prospect to upload their current export and test the workflow during the 14-day trial.

## 6. Discovery questions

1. How do you currently review sales performance every week?
2. Where does the data live?
3. How long does the weekly/monthly report take to prepare?
4. Which revenue question is hardest to answer quickly?
5. What happens when the forecast is wrong?
6. Who is responsible for explaining missed targets?
7. What would make an analyst tool worth paying for?
8. What stops you from using existing CRM reporting?
9. What data would you never want a third party to handle?
10. What result would make you continue after the trial?

## 7. Qualification signal

A prospect is high-fit when there is measurable pipeline, recurring management review, structured sales data, multiple sellers, a clear revenue owner, and a spreadsheet/reporting pain with an economic cost.

Disqualify early when there is no usable sales data, no sales process, or no economic reason to monitor revenue performance.

## 8. Outreach sequence

No CRM replacement.

I’m looking for a small number of Dubai/UAE companies to use it with real data and pressure-test the workflow.

Would you be open to a 15-minute walkthrough using a sanitized export?

Happy to walk through a sanitized example.

### Message 3 — close the loop

Hi {{name}},

Closing the loop here.

We’re testing the product with a small number of UAE B2B teams that already have pipeline data but limited analyst/RevOps capacity.

The trial is designed around one practical question: can the tool make the next revenue review faster and better informed?

## 9. Activation events to instrument

Track first dataset upload, second/refreshed dataset upload, first analyst question, repeat analyst question, first saved intelligence item, first monitoring rule, report generation, teammate/seat interest, billing-page visit, upgrade attempt, and paid conversion.

North-star candidate: Time-to-Decision = time from usable data availability to the first evidence-backed business action accepted by the user.

## 10. Operating cadence

Daily: contact a small focused batch of qualified accounts, run discovery calls, review activation and objections, and keep the demo path simple.

Weekly: summarize activation funnel, classify objections, identify the strongest recurring use case/vertical, and update landing/outreach language from observed customer language.

Do not scale acquisition materially until activation, repeat analysis, trial conversion, and customer-reported value are measurable.

## 11. Guardrails

Use sanitized data unless the prospect explicitly provides data under an appropriate agreement.

Do not promise integrations that are not implemented.

Do not claim forecasts beyond what the uploaded evidence supports.

Do not imply that AI replaces a CRM.

Keep implementation requirements close to zero during the beachhead.

Keep the operating model automation-heavy because the business is intentionally run by one founder/operator plus the AI engineering agent.
